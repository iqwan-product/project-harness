# Contributing

This kit grows from incidents, not from ideas.

## What earns a place

Three things must be true:

- Something failed.
- The cause is understood.
- The correction generalises past the project it happened in.

Corrections specific to one stack belong in that project's own
documentation. The kit stays stack-agnostic: no language, framework,
database, or cloud provider.

## How to write it

Every item states what it prevents, not only what to do. An item
without its reason gets dropped by the next reader, correctly,
because applicability cannot be assessed.

Register is flat technical reference. No first person, no narrative,
no marketing.

## Removals

Removals follow the same discipline and are equally welcome. Where an
item no longer earns its place, cut it and say why.

## Pull requests

Use the format in `templates/pr-description-template.md`. What
changed, why, how to verify, and what it could break.
